# About Elvis Calcano

Some stories are discovered.

Others are inherited.

I grew up fascinated by the history of the Caribbean—the age of sail, the great pirate legends, the forgotten kingdoms of the Taíno people, and the mysteries hidden beyond the horizon.

Those passions eventually became the foundation of the **Pirate Tales Saga**, a historical fantasy adventure series that blends real Caribbean history with supernatural mysteries, legendary treasures, cursed seas, and unforgettable voyages.

My goal has always been simple:

To create the kind of stories I wanted to read—stories where pirates are more than villains, where history and myth collide, and where every island hides a secret waiting to be uncovered.

---

## The Pirate Tales Saga

The Pirate Tales Saga follows sailors, adventurers, rebels, and survivors as they navigate a Caribbean filled with danger, mystery, and ancient powers.

### Published Books

### Book I — The Secret Treasure

A quest for a legendary treasure leads to discoveries that will change the fate of every sailor involved.

### Book II — Islas de los Muertos

Strange islands emerge from the sea, forgotten horrors awaken, and survival becomes uncertain.

### Book III — The Assault of Santo Domingo

Pirates, soldiers, and powerful empires collide in a struggle that threatens the future of the Caribbean.

### Book IV — The Captain's Curse

Ancient forces return, old enemies rise, and a curse begins to reshape the destiny of the seas.

---

## Inspirations

The Pirate Tales Saga draws inspiration from:

* Caribbean history
* The Age of Sail
* Pirate folklore
* Taíno mythology
* Colonial Santo Domingo
* Maritime exploration
* Supernatural legends and sea mysteries

While many locations and historical elements are real, the stories themselves combine history and imagination to create a unique adventure world.
