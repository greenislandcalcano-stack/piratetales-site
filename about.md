# About Elvis Calcano

## Author • Storyteller • Caribbean History Enthusiast

Elvis Calcano is the creator and author of the **Pirate Tales Saga**, a historical fantasy adventure series that blends Caribbean history, pirate folklore, Taíno mythology, supernatural mysteries, and epic sea voyages.

The Pirate Tales universe began its journey in **2019**, when the original manuscript was formally registered under the title *Pirate Tale*. Since then, the project has expanded into a growing multi-book saga exploring the hidden legends, forgotten kingdoms, cursed waters, and untold histories of the Caribbean.

---

## Why Pirate Tales?

The Caribbean is one of the most fascinating regions in world history.

Long before modern tourism and cruise ships, these waters were home to Taíno kingdoms, Spanish conquistadors, privateers, pirates, hidden settlements, lost treasures, and countless legends that still survive today.

The Pirate Tales Saga was created to capture that spirit of adventure while weaving together historical inspiration and imaginative storytelling.

Readers will discover:

* Pirate captains and sea battles
* Ancient Taíno legends
* Colonial Caribbean intrigue
* Lost treasures and forgotten maps
* Haunted islands and supernatural mysteries
* Dangerous voyages across uncharted waters

---

## The Pirate Tales Saga

### Book I — The Secret Treasure

A legendary treasure draws sailors, adventurers, and dreamers into a dangerous journey that will change their lives forever.

### Book II — Islas de los Muertos

Mysterious islands emerge from the sea as ancient horrors awaken beneath forgotten waters.

### Book III — The Assault of Santo Domingo

Pirates, soldiers, governors, and rebels collide in a struggle that threatens the future of the Caribbean itself.

### Book IV — The Captain's Curse

A dark curse begins to spread across the seas, forcing old allies and enemies to confront forces older than history.

---

## Inspired by the Caribbean

Many locations featured throughout the saga draw inspiration from real places throughout the Dominican Republic and the wider Caribbean, including:

* Samaná Bay
* Los Haitises
* Colonial Santo Domingo
* Tortuga
* Hispaniola
* Historic Caribbean trade routes

While the stories are works of fiction, they are deeply influenced by the culture, history, and legends of the region.

---

## Building a Living World

The Pirate Tales Saga is more than a collection of books.

It is an expanding fictional universe filled with recurring characters, historical mysteries, ancient powers, forgotten civilizations, legendary ships, and interconnected adventures that span generations.

New stories, lore articles, character profiles, maps, and historical explorations continue to be added as the world grows.
